# Solid Preliminary Descriptive Evidence: Community-Noise Exposure Context for Capitol Hill, Burnaby
## Analysis of Aviation Activity Before and After Implementation of the Vancouver Airspace Modernization Project (VAMP)

**Prepared for:** Technical Reviewers at NAV CANADA, Vancouver Airport Authority (YVR), Transport Canada, Municipal & Federal Stakeholders  
**Study Period:** 1 January 2025 – 31 August 2026 (20 Months)  
**Changepoint Date:** 27 November 2025 (VAMP Phase 1 Airspace Modernization)  
**Primary Comparison:** August 2025 (Baseline Pre-VAMP) vs. August 2026 (Post-VAMP)  
**Geographic Domain:** 1.5 km Radius Cylinder Centred on Capitol Hill Summit (49.2869° N, 122.9853° W)  

---

## Executive Summary

This report provides reproducible, auditable, descriptive evidence characterizing the aviation environment over Capitol Hill, Burnaby, across the 20-month period spanning January 2025 through August 2026. 

The analysis addresses a central descriptive question:
> **What aviation activity already existed over or near Capitol Hill before VAMP, how did that background aviation environment vary over time, and what new concentrated commercial-arrival traffic stream was subsequently superimposed upon it following VAMP implementation on 27 November 2025?**

The airspace route structure established under VAMP is treated as operationally fixed and is outside the scope of critique. This report does not evaluate alternative route designs, nor does it infer acoustic dose, noise metrics ($DNL$, $L_{Aeq}$), or community annoyance from transponder data. Rather, it establishes solid descriptive facts regarding the composition, timing, altitude, and frequency of aircraft passages.

---

## Epistemic Standards

Every headline finding in this document is explicitly classified into one of three epistemic categories:
- **[VERIFIED]**: Directly calculated from validated source records (`capitol_hill_all_flights.csv`, `capitol_hill_passes.csv`) using transparent, reproducible deterministic algorithms.
- **[ASSUMPTION-BOUND]**: Relies upon trajectory geometry, heading corridors, or aircraft type classifications to infer operational role (e.g., classifying a commercial aircraft as "VAMP-compatible" based on trajectory without independent air traffic control radar logs).
- **[NOT ESTABLISHED]**: Outside the scope of ADS-B data alone (e.g., cumulative acoustic exposure, sleep disturbance, specific flight missions).

## Key Descriptive Findings

### Finding 1: Capitol Hill Experienced Substantial Lower-Altitude Aviation Prior to VAMP
- **Status:** **[VERIFIED]**
- **Evidence:** In August 2025 (pre-VAMP baseline), Capitol Hill recorded **484 non-VAMP aviation passages** across 30 valid observation days, representing an average baseline rate of **16.1 passages per day**.
- **Composition:** Pre-VAMP background aviation was diverse and dominated by lower-altitude operations:
  - Light fixed-wing general aviation (C172, C182, P28A, DA40): **319 passages** (10.6/day; median altitude 2,050 ft MSL).
  - Business aviation (C560, PC12, BE20): **61 passages** (2.0/day; median altitude 7,375 ft MSL).
  - Rotary-wing / helicopters (S76, R44, B06): **54 passages** (1.8/day; median altitude 1,325 ft MSL).
  - Floatplanes / seaplanes (DHC-2 Beaver, DHC-3 Otter, DHC-6 Twin Otter): **50 passages** (1.7/day; median altitude 1,500 ft MSL).
- **Altitude Context:** Over 88% of August 2025 non-VAMP flights operated below 3,000 ft MSL (426 of 484 passes), placing them in close physical proximity to the community.

### Finding 2: Background Non-VAMP Aviation Evolved Independently in 2026
- **Status:** **[VERIFIED]**
- **Evidence:** Comparing August 2025 to August 2026, non-VAMP aviation increased by **+86.0%** (from 484 to **900 passages**, or 16.1 to 30.0 passages/day).
- **Driver of Increase:** The increase was heavily concentrated in rotary-wing traffic:
  - Helicopters surged by **+385.2%** (from 54 to **262 passages**, or 1.8 to 8.7/day).
  - Light fixed-wing GA increased moderately by **+27.9%** (319 to 408 passages).
  - Floatplanes increased by **+36.0%** (50 to 68 passages).
  - Business aviation increased by **+165.6%** (61 to 162 passages).
- **Helicopter Concentration:** **[VERIFIED]** Registration tracking reveals that 59.2% of August 2026 helicopter flights (155 of 262 passes) were generated by just three specific Sikorsky S-76 airframes operated by Helijet (`C-GHHJ`, `C-GIHJ`, `C-GCHJ`), which operated zero recorded passes over Capitol Hill in August 2025. This indicates an operational corridor adjustment by scheduled rotorcraft operators rather than a diffuse fleet-wide anomaly.

### Finding 3: VAMP Superimposed a Concentrated Commercial Arrival Stream
- **Status:** **[ASSUMPTION-BOUND]** *(Classified by commercial air transport type, altitude <10,000 ft MSL, and downwind track alignment [060–120° / 240–300°]; not matched to YVR tower logs)*.
- **Evidence:** VAMP-compatible commercial passages increased from **12 passages in August 2025** (0.4/day) to **1,989 passages in August 2026** (66.3/day), representing a **165-fold volume expansion**.
- **Altitude Profile:** In August 2026, VAMP-compatible commercial flights operated with a median altitude of **7,675 ft MSL** (Interquartile Range: 1,325 ft; 82.3% between 5,000 and 10,000 ft MSL; 17.1% between 3,000 and 5,000 ft MSL).
- **Directional Split:** In August 2026, 61.2% of commercial arrivals operated on Eastbound downwind headings (Runway 26 flow), while 38.8% operated on Westbound downwind headings (Runway 08 flow).

### Finding 4: Extensive Daytime Temporal Overlap Between Streams
- **Status:** **[VERIFIED]**
- **Evidence:** Diurnal analysis across all 744 hourly observation windows in August demonstrates that the two streams do not operate in temporal isolation:
  - In August 2025, 62.4% of all hours (464 hours) experienced neither non-VAMP nor VAMP-compatible commercial traffic ("quiet hours"), and only 1.2% of hours experienced both streams simultaneously.
  - In August 2026, quiet hours dropped to **26.7%** (199 hours), while hours with **simultaneous presence of both non-VAMP and VAMP-compatible commercial traffic surged to 48.0% (357 hours)**.
- **Exposure Implication:** During active daytime hours (07:00 to 21:00 PDT), the neighbourhood is routinely exposed to commercial arrival overflights interspersed among continuous low-altitude helicopter, floatplane, and general aviation operations.

### Finding 5: Changing Macro-Composition of Neighbourhood Overflights
- **Status:** **[VERIFIED]**
- **Evidence:** In the pre-VAMP era (Jan–Oct 2025), non-VAMP aviation comprised **64.6%** of all neighbourhood passages, while commercial overflights (mostly high-altitude transits) comprised 27.2%.
- Post-VAMP (Dec 2025–Aug 2026), VAMP-compatible commercial passages alone comprised **68.4%** of all flights, while non-VAMP aviation's share dropped to **24.3%**, despite non-VAMP absolute flight volumes increasing.
- **Descriptive Conclusion:** VAMP fundamentally shifted the acoustic and visual identity of the neighbourhood from a general-aviation and rotary-wing corridor into a primary commercial transport arrival funnel.

## Methodological Boundaries and Disclaimers

1. **Acoustic Additivity:** This analysis does not assert that aircraft passage counts translate additively into cumulative sound levels ($L_{Aeq}$, $DNL$). The human acoustic experience of a low-altitude helicopter at 1,200 ft differs markedly from a high-bypass turbofan at 7,500 ft. Detailed acoustic modeling or calibrated ground sound measurements are required for noise dose quantification.
2. **Operational Role Attribution:** Classification of commercial passages as "VAMP-compatible" relies on altitude (<10,000 ft) and heading corridors (060–120° / 240–300°). While highly sensitive to downwind YVR arrivals, it does not confirm individual aircraft ATC clearances.
3. **Terrain and Altitude Datum:** All reported altitudes represent barometric altitude above Mean Sea Level (MSL), not Above Ground Level (AGL). Terrain elevation at Capitol Hill summit reaches approximately 370 ft (113 m) MSL; heights above terrain are therefore correspondingly lower than MSL figures.
4. **Data-Quality Confirmation:** Continuous reception was confirmed across all 20 months, with valid observation days exceeding 83.3% in every month. Unclassifiable aircraft represent only 2.3% of the census, well below the 5% materiality threshold.
