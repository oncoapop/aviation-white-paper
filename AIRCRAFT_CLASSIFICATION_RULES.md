# Aircraft Classification Rules and Operational Categories

## 1. Regulatory Context and Tri-Partite Classification

To ensure regulator-defensible descriptive precision, all aircraft operations traversing the 1.5 km study cylinder around Capitol Hill are classified into three mutually exclusive primary operational classes:

### Category 1: Non-VAMP Aviation
- **Governing Principle:** Class membership is based strictly on operational category and study geography, **NOT on a hard <5,000 ft cutoff**. The <5,000 ft MSL threshold is maintained solely as a descriptive altitude stratum.
- **Operational Sub-Classes:**
  - **Light Fixed-Wing GA:** Single- and twin-engine light piston/turboprop aircraft (e.g., C172, C182, P28A, DA40) primarily operating out of Boundary Bay (CZBB), Pitt Meadows (CYPK), or Langley (CYNJ).
  - **Helicopters:** Rotary-wing aircraft (e.g., S76, R44, B06, AS50, A139, EC35). Prominently includes scheduled passenger service (Helijet), emergency medical services (BCCN / Air Ambulance), police patrols, and private rotorcraft.
  - **Floatplanes / Seaplanes:** Fixed-wing amphibious/float aircraft (DHC-2 Beaver, DHC-3 Otter, DHC-6 Twin Otter, DH2T, DH3T) operating scheduled and charter passenger routes from Vancouver Harbour (CYHC) and the Fraser River (CAM9).
  - **Business Aviation:** Business jets and corporate turboprops (e.g., C560, PC12, BE20, CL60) operating general/corporate aviation profiles.

### Category 2: VAMP-Compatible Commercial Passages
- **Governing Principle:** Defined by commercial aircraft type (`commercial`), altitude $< 10,000$ ft MSL, horizontal CPA distance $\le 1.5$ km, and heading aligned with the Eastbound ($060-120^\circ$) or Westbound ($240-300^\circ$) downwind arrival streams.
- **CRITICAL EPISTEMIC INJUNCTION:** This classification is a **geometric/trajectory proxy** and **MUST NEVER be relabelled as a confirmed YVR arrival** unless independently verified by official ATC flight plan records or radar logs. Label: **ASSUMPTION-BOUND**.

### Category 3: Other / Unknown Traffic
- Commercial transport aircraft transiting at or above 10,000 ft MSL, or commercial aircraft operating on non-downwind headings (e.g., pre-VAMP northbound cross-traffic at 7,000–9,000 ft).
- Rare, unclassifiable, military, or missing type codes.
