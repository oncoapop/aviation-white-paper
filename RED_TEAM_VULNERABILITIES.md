# Red-Team Vulnerability Assessment and Technical Defense
## Antagonistic Review from the Perspective of a NAV CANADA / YVR Airspace Analyst

This document evaluates the principal claims of the Capitol Hill exposure-context analysis against potential challenges from air navigation service provider (ANSP) and airport authority technical analysts. Every claim is analyzed for evidence robustness, the strongest potential counterargument, the quantitative analysis addressing that counterargument, and the residual limitations that must be conceded.

## Strategic Guidance for White-Paper Authors

1. **Avoid Route Design Debates:** If YVR or NAV CANADA analysts argue that the Burnaby arrival corridor is required for PBN (Performance-Based Navigation) efficiency or safety, **do not challenge the aeronautical design**. The white paper's scope is purely exposure context: documenting the physical reality of what traffic was added to what existing baseline.
2. **Never Claim Certified Runway Assignment:** Because the dataset does not incorporate NAV CANADA EXCDS or flight plan records, always refer to arrival streams as *"VAMP-compatible commercial downwind passages"* rather than *"confirmed Runway 26L/08R arrivals."*
3. **Acknowledge Local Baseline Growth:** Do not attempt to minimize the 2026 increase in helicopters or general aviation. Fully disclosing that non-VAMP aviation grew from 16.1 to 30.0 passes/day demonstrates methodological integrity and disarms the critique that the study is biased against NAV CANADA.
